Progress log

Runs of the velocity setup study up to 20 September 2026, with the solver time of each run.

The runs come in pairs with the same code, level and contrast. One run of each pair starts with the sharp velocity edge at 1.3 cloud radii that the paper uses, the other with the earlier velocity setup of that code, so the two histories differ only in the starting velocity. The runs of the paper are on the Runs page, and the research page explains them.

60 runs of 12 methods, 6,066 saved states and 12.3 h of solver time in total.
