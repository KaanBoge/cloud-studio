Honest time estimates

Live completion uses simulated time, not a count of images. Remaining time is estimated from recent native progress and excludes later validation and analysis. No estimate is shown when evidence is insufficient.

Low overhead by design

The page is hosted online. A small sleeping publisher reads bounded metadata about once a minute and uploads only a compact report. Idle uploads are less frequent. Close this tab or view it on a phone to avoid browser work on the simulation PC.

No stale “running” labels

An old process number is not evidence of a running simulation. The publisher checks its registered controller identity. Missing updates are labelled stale, and their live progress and remaining time are withheld.
