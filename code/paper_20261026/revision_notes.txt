Concrete scientific findings
- The finest GIZMO input uses a uniform lattice with variable masses. Its preparation
  record reports 8,808 centres inside the cloud radius. This differs from the
  denser equal-mass cloud sampling used in the Braspenning comparison.
- Wind-direction boundary conditions differ between recorded code configurations.
- GADGET-4 has measured initial reconstructed pressure excesses. Its curves are
  explicitly marked as requiring corrected initial-pressure controls.
- MFV's dense-mass resolution trend is non-monotonic; the present ladder does not
  support a convergence claim. Similar covering fractions coexist with different
  dense masses, so the diagnostics must be interpreted together.

Scope of reproduction
plot_revision.py regenerates three figures from retained scalar reductions. It
does not rerun evolution, completion validators, raw hashes or raw-field analysis.
Other figure PDFs are retained from the earlier completed analysis. The overview
header alone was changed from the internal L4 label to n=64.

The manuscript is a working scientific draft, not a submission-ready claim that
all code configurations are identical or that a preferred solver is established.
