About the surfaces

Each frame is one saved state of the simulation. The two surfaces enclose gas denser than 0.15 and 0.6 of the initial cloud density, so the inner surface traces the cloud core.

Lengths are in cloud radii and the wind blows along the long side of the box. Smooth transitions crossfade between saved states during playback; paused views and pictures show a saved state exactly as stored.
