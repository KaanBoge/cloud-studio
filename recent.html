What is shown and how to use it

These are projections of total gas column density, normalized by the initial cloud density and radius: Σ / (ρc,0 R). The fixed logarithmic magma scale, 0.008–5, matches the paper. Edge-on places the wind left to right; face-on looks along the wind. Coordinates use the initial cloud radius. Particle runs are binned into 128 × 256 and 128 × 128 maps and translated into the same plotting coordinates.

Each animation contains 11 real saved states sampled at approximately 0.5 cloud-crushing times, from 0 to 5. It holds each saved state on screen; it does not synthesize intervening fluid motion. The original runs retain their finer output cadence. PNG and video exports use the selected view, comparison and labels. Video is recorded locally in your browser; supported format depends on the browser. No simulation is running in this page.

Every projection was checked against the accepted saved mass and dense-mass reductions. Numerical acceptance does not establish convergence or rank the codes by physical accuracy. Boundary conditions, initial particle construction, transport choices and correction source differ between some runs; the selected run’s notes describe these limits. All original simulation files and backups are preserved.
