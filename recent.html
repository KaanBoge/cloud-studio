How to read the pictures

Each picture is a projection of the total gas column density, divided by the initial cloud density and radius: Σ / (ρc,0 R). The colour scale is logarithmic, from 0.008 to 5, the same as in the paper. Edge on places the wind from left to right; face on looks along the wind. Distances are in units of the initial cloud radius. Particle and moving mesh runs are binned onto the same 256 × 128 and 128 × 128 maps as the grid runs.

Every frame is a saved state of the run, and every picture prints its time as t = 1.25 tcc. When two runs are compared or a set of runs stands side by side, each panel shows its own saved state nearest to the chosen time and prints that time. Side by side, the view Edge on and face on places each edge on map next to its face on map, as in the paper. Smooth playback blends neighbouring frames at 60 frames per second; when playback stops, the picture is a saved state again. Saved pictures and videos use the chosen view, comparison or set, and labels. Space plays and pauses, the arrow keys step one saved state, Home and End go to the first and last.

The pictures are made directly from the saved simulation output, as described under About the data.
